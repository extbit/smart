# Commercial Services & Custom Plugin Development

While the core engine of **Smart Make Art (smart)** is free and open-source under the BSD 3-Clause License, **ExtBit LLC** offers commercial development, priority SLA support, and custom build-automation services for engineering teams and businesses.

---

## Service Offerings

### 1. Custom Plugin & Recipe Development
We engineer and license closed-source or proprietary extensions tailored to your internal software stack:
* **Proprietary Toolchain Integrations:** Custom JIT dialects and runners for internal compilers, embedded toolchains, or custom synthesis utilities.
* **Advanced Build Caching:** Distributed compilation caching modules designed to accelerate build speeds across multi-developer teams.
* **CI/CD Pipeline Extensions:** Tailored runners, telemetry plugins, and artifact hooks for GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Docker, or custom build infrastructure.

### 2. Build Automation & Migration Consulting
Modernize legacy build processes without disrupting active development workflows:
* **Makefile Refactoring:** Refactoring large, variable-polluted Makefiles into modular, scoped `smart` modules.
* **Monorepo Architecture:** Designing multi-dialect build graphs for C/C++, Golang, or multi-language projects (like LLVM or Bitcoin Core extensions).
* **Compile-Time Optimization:** Profiling compiler flags, dependency graphs, and parallel execution bottlenecks.

### 3. Enterprise SLAs & Priority Support
Dedicated support contracts for teams running `smart` in production build chains:
* Guaranteed response times for build-blocking defect resolution.
* Direct technical access to principal systems architects via dedicated Slack or Teams channels.
* Custom feature prioritization and scheduled security advisories.
