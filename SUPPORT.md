# Getting Support for Smart

Thank you for using **Smart Make Art (smart)**! Depending on your operational requirements, we offer both community-driven open-source support and dedicated commercial assistance provided by **ExtBit LLC**.

---

## Community Support (Free)

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis by project maintainers and open-source contributors:

* **Documentation & Wiki:** Read the [Official Documentation](https://github.com/extbit/smart/wiki) for guides on JIT syntax, recipe creation, and module architecture.
* **Bug Reports & Feature Requests:** Search existing [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/extbit/smart/issues) first. If your issue hasn't been reported, open a new issue with a minimal reproducible example, your OS/architecture details, and the output of `smart -version`.
* **Community Chat:** Join our discussion channel on [Gitter](https://gitter.im/duzy/smart) for technical discussions, syntax feedback, and general questions.

---

## Commercial & Priority Support

If you rely on `smart` inside a commercial product, mission-critical build pipeline, or enterprise CI/CD setup, community support turnaround times may not align with your operational needs.

**ExtBit LLC** provides formal commercial support, custom plugin engineering, and direct pipeline consulting:

* **Priority Bug Fixes:** Direct patch delivery and SLA-backed resolution times for build-blocking issues.
* **Custom Plugin Engineering:** Development of proprietary dialects, hardware toolchain integrations, and custom build caching.
* **Build System Migration:** Hands-on refactoring of complex nested Makefiles and fragile compile scripts.
